Biography & Life

A life shaped by learning, leadership, and public duty.

CM Abdulla Moulavi, widely remembered as CM Usthad and Chembirika Qazi, was a Kerala Muslim scholar known for his command of Islamic jurisprudence, astronomy, mathematics, languages, and community leadership.

His public life connected traditional scholarship with careful scientific inquiry. He wrote and taught on prayer time calculation, qibla direction, moon sighting, literature, and religious education, while also helping build institutions that carried his educational vision forward.

His vision was to synthesize the eternal values of faith with the progressive tools of science, mathematics, and institutional excellence.

Scholarly Formation

Jamia Sa'diyya Arabia Established

Jamia Sa'diyya Arabia started as a premier educational institution, marking a major milestone in advancing Islamic learning and social welfare in Kasaragod.

Institution
STEP 04 1974

Appointed Qazi for the First Time

Following the passing of his father, CM Usthad was appointed for the first time as Qazi, assuming judicial leadership for Chembirika and surrounding mahals.

Judicial Leadership
STEP 05 1993

Foundation Laid for Malabar Islamic Complex (MIC)

Foundation was laid for the Malabar Islamic Complex (MIC) in Chattanchal, creating a campus integrating Islamic jurisprudence with modern academic education.

MIC Foundation
STEP 06 2008

Appointed Qazi of Mangalore

Appointed as the Chief Qazi of Mangalore (South Karnataka), extending his judicial and spiritual leadership across major mahals in Karnataka and Kerala.

Mangalore Qazi
STEP 07 15 Feb 2010

Martyred on 15 February 2010

CM Usthad was martyred on 15 February 2010. His martyrdom inspired widespread community solidarity and a continuing pursuit of truth, justice, and archive preservation.
